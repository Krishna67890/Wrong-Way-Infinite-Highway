# 🚗 Highway Havoc: Wrong Way

### Drive Against Traffic. Dodge Danger. Survive the Highway!

Welcome to **Highway Havoc: Wrong Way**, an exciting endless highway arcade driving game developed by **Krishna Ajaysing Patil**, under the brand **Krishna Patil Rajput**.

Take control of your car, drive against traffic, dodge incoming vehicles, collect rewards and see how long you can survive on a dangerous six-lane highway!

---

## 🎮 About the Game

Highway Havoc: Wrong Way is an arcade-style driving game that challenges your reflexes, timing and driving skills.

Navigate through busy traffic, avoid collisions, manage your vehicle's health and aim for the highest possible score.

Every second counts. One wrong move can end your run!

## ✨ Game Features

- 🛣️ **Six-Lane Highway:** Navigate through a challenging multi-lane road.
- 🚘 **Multiple Cars:** Choose from different vehicles.
- 🏎️ **Endless Gameplay:** Drive as far as possible and beat your records.
- 💥 **Vehicle Damage System:** Watch your vehicle's health and avoid crashes.
- ❤️ **Health & Healing:** Use available healing pickups to survive longer.
- 🤖 **Traffic Bots:** Face moving traffic and challenging road situations.
- 🚗 **Car Garage:** Unlock new vehicles using in-game currency.
- 🪙 **In-Game Economy:** Earn and spend rewards.
- 🌆 **Highway Environment:** Enjoy an exciting road-driving experience.
- 📱 **Android Gameplay:** Designed for mobile devices.
- 🎯 **Reflex Challenges:** Test your reaction speed and driving skills.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Start the game and enter the highway.
2. Control your vehicle using the available game controls.
3. Dodge incoming traffic and avoid collisions.
4. Keep an eye on your vehicle's health.
5. Collect available rewards and pickups.
6. Unlock additional cars from the garage.
7. Survive as long as possible and improve your score.
